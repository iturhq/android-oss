== Preconditions

* App is in the Ongoing state with the *organiser* role.
* At least one participant has sent a hail (i.e., `FakeActivityRepository` has a pending attention request).

== Steps — acknowledge one hail

. Observe the in-app notification indicating that a participant is hailing.
. Expand the notification to reveal the full list of hailing participants.
. Tap *Acknowledge* next to one participant.
. Observe the notification list.

== Expected result — acknowledge one hail

* The acknowledged participant is removed from the list.
* If other participants are still hailing, the notification remains with the remaining entries.
* The acknowledged participant's "Hail organiser" FAB is restored to its default colour on their device (verified via `FakeActivityRepository` state).
* No crash occurs.

== Steps — acknowledge all hails

. With multiple participants hailing, tap *Acknowledge all* in the notification.
. Observe the notification.

== Expected result — acknowledge all hails

* The notification list is cleared entirely.
* All hailing participants' "Hail organiser" FABs are restored to their default colour (verified via `FakeActivityRepository` state).
* No crash occurs.
