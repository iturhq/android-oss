= UC-13: Organizer displays QR code
:icons: font

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| ID | UC-13
| Path type | Positive
| Feature | xref:../features.adoc#_feature_3_organise_an_activity[Feature 3 — Organise an activity]
|===

== Preconditions

* App is in the Ongoing state with the *organizer* role.
* The ongoing activity has a valid ID.

== Steps

. Verify *Show QR* FAB is visible and *Hail organiser* FAB is absent.
. Tap *Show QR* FAB.
. Observe the QR Display bottom sheet.
. Verify the QR image is rendered (non-blank).
. Dismiss the sheet by tapping outside it or swiping down.

== Expected result

* Bottom sheet appears with title `Scan this QR to join the activity`.
* QR image is rendered and visible (non-null bitmap).
* After dismissal, the Ongoing overlay is restored.
* *Show QR* FAB is still visible.
* No error occurs.

| `contentDescription = "Show QR"`

== Notes

Visual verification of QR content correctness requires screenshot comparison or a separate QR decode step in addition to the selector assertions.
